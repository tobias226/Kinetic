- The app does not claim that Copley Point or any real past partner uses this scoring, pursues any of the illustrative opportunity themes or has a relationship to the fictional parties.
- Source facts are not automatically refreshed. Dates on the Market Signals page are provided to prevent stale information appearing live.
- The carbon calculator uses editable example numbers, not extracted reference data or a reproduction of Copley Point's model.
